Gunnery Primer — how shells, terrain & armour interact

CITY BATTLE replaces the open sea with real topography. One rule unifies all of it: the angle a shell falls at decides which armour face it strikes — flat shots hit the thick side/glacis, plunging shots hit the thin top/deck. Terrain decides what you can even reach. Step through the cards below (←/→ keys or the buttons). The red-framed cards are the make-or-break ideas.
