# CITY BATTLE — Game Wiki

The reference for how CITY BATTLE works — for design consistency and as a new-player guide.
CITY BATTLE is **Rule the Waves on land**: artillery **crab-mechas** (walking warships) fought
across **real city terrain** (Sydney first), with deep ship-style design, research, and a
victory-point / prestige campaign.

## Design north star
Faithful to **Rule the Waves 2/3** mechanics (see `docs/wiki/ref/`), re-themed to land artillery
with **real US Army artillery doctrine** for the terrain/trajectory layer. One unifying ballistic
rule drives it all: **angle of fall vs the armour facing presented** — flat fire hits the
side/glacis, plunging fire hits the top/deck.
